MAIN CHARACTER — a cat named Momo. Momo appears in EVERY scene.
- Short-haired grey mackerel tabby. Round body, short legs.
- White only on the chest and the tips of all four paws. Pale pink nose.
- Big round amber eyes with vertical slit pupils.
- The tip of the LEFT ear is slightly folded — this is how Momo is recognised across episodes.
- A navy fabric collar with one small wooden name tag.
- Always calm and a little unimpressed. Small mouth shaped like ω.
- About palm-sized on screen. The main character, but never larger than the thing being explained.
- Never change any of the above between scenes or episodes: same colours, stripes, folded ear, collar.
- Momo looks at, points to or touches the thing being explained. Momo never looks straight at the camera.
No humans. If a hand is needed, Momo's front paw does the job.
